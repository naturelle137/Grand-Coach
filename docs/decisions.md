## D-03 Kontra/Re in detail

1. **Who may say Kontra:** each defender who has bid or held at least 18.
   Usual exception for rear hand (Hinterhand) if fore hand (Vorhand) and
   middle hand (Mittelhand) have already bid beyond 18.
2. **When:** after the game announcement and before the defender in question
   plays their first card. Only one Kontra per game.
3. **Re:** only the declarer, and only immediately in reply to Kontra. No
   further escalation.
4. **Scoring:** Kontra doubles and Kontra plus Re quadruples the regular game
   points, including loss points that may already be doubled. The bid value
   and the overbid check are unaffected. With Seeger-Fabian the extra
   +50/−50 and +40 points are not multiplied.
5. **Game types:** Kontra and Re are allowed in suit, Grand and Null games,
   including Hand games. In open games the announcements must be settled
   before the cards are revealed. Unlike some pub rule profiles, Re is also
   allowed in Null and Null Hand.

## D-26 Kontra/Re clarified

1. **Eligibility:** a defender may say Kontra if they bid or held at least 18.
   Rear hand (Hinterhand) may also say Kontra if, at its first turn in the
   bidding, the standing bid was already above 18 and it passed.
2. **Timing:** an eligible defender may say Kontra instead of playing a card
   while it is their turn and they have not played a card yet. Only one Kontra
   per game.
3. **Re:** immediately after Kontra the declarer decides Re or no Re before
   anything else happens; then play continues.
4. **Ouvert:** before the declarer's cards are revealed, each eligible
   defender decides once whether to say Kontra, followed by the declarer's Re
   decision if needed.

## D-28 Rule interpretations in `skat-core`

Sources: the Internationale Skatordnung (ISkO), Altenburg 2002 edition
(PDF: <https://www.spielkarten.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/0_Skat_InternationaleSkatordnung.pdf>),
read through its English translation
(<https://www.weddslist.com/skat/ordnung/skatordnung3.html>). Section numbers
below refer to the ISkO.

1. **Bidding:** bids must be values from the Reizwert table (5.6.1); jumps
   are allowed. If Mittelhand and Hinterhand pass without a bid, Vorhand
   either plays at 18 (`Bid(18)`, no other value) or passes (3.3.6); three
   passes throw the hand in (3.3.7, D-04).
2. **Null below the bid:** a Null game whose fixed value is below the bid
   cannot be announced; the declarer must announce a suit or Grand game
   instead (3.4.4). The engine rejects it with `NullValueBelowBid`.
3. **Lost and overbid games:** every lost game counts double (5.3.4). An
   overbid game is lost with the smallest multiple of its base value that
   reaches the bid, then doubled (5.4.1, 5.4.2).
4. **Levels:** announced Schneider/Schwarz always count their levels.
   Schneider (either party at 30 Augen or fewer) and Schwarz (either party
   without a trick) count when reached. A declarer who announced Schneider
   is played Schneider gets no extra level (5.2.5).
5. **End of play:** suit and Grand games are played to the tenth trick;
   Null ends as soon as the declarer takes a trick. Conceding and shortening
   (4.3) are not modelled in M1.
6. **Ouvert Kontra round (D-26.4):** eligible defenders decide in seat order
   Vorhand, Mittelhand, Hinterhand; after a Kontra the round ends with the
   declarer's Re decision. In Ouvert play no Kontra is possible later.
7. **Seats and scoring:** `skat-core` uses per-deal seats (Vorhand,
   Mittelhand, Hinterhand = dealer). Seeger-Fabian contributions are returned
   per seat; mapping seats to players belongs to the series layer.
8. **Dealing:** Fisher–Yates shuffle with rejection sampling on the
   `ChaCha8Rng` stream (seeded with `seed_from_u64`), dealt 3 – Skat 2 – 4 – 3
   from Vorhand, so a seed always gives the same deal.
9. **Fixed-deal coverage:** each variant is played through (bidding,
   announcement, cards, settlement) with hand-computed results in
   `crates/skat-core/tests/`. `variants.rs` holds Grand (pickup, Hand,
   Schneider/Schwarz announced, Ouvert), Herz and Pik (Hand, announced
   levels, Ouvert), Schneider/Schwarz announced and made, Schneider announced
   and missed with 88 Augen, Schwarz announced and missed, Ouvert lost, Karo
   Hand lost with every announcement, Überreizen, and Null (Hand, Ouvert,
   Ouvert Hand, won and lost). `play.rs` covers Kreuz with pickup and Hand,
   Karo with pickup, Null with pickup and Null ending early; `kontra.rs`
   covers Kontra/Re.

## D-31 Series semantics

1. **Players and seats:** the series has three players. In each deal the
   dealer is Hinterhand, the player to the dealer's left Vorhand. Player 2
   deals the first game, so the human starts as Vorhand.
2. **Rotation:** after every deal, also a thrown-in one, the deal passes to
   the left.
3. **Einpassen:** if all pass, the deal is thrown in (D-04). It counts as a
   game of the series (a line on the score sheet with no points), as in
   tournament lists; the next player deals.
4. **Scoring:** each line holds the Seeger-Fabian points per player as
   computed by `skat-core` (`GameResult::seeger_fabian`, mapped from seats to
   players) and the running totals. The series result lists totals, games
   won and lost as declarer, and the ranking by total.
5. **Length:** a fixed series ends after its n-th game (default 36). In free
   play the human ends the series between games.
6. **Seeds:** the series seed seeds a `ChaCha8Rng` that draws one seed per
   deal; the same series seed and the same human commands repeat the series.
